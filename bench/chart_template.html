What was tested, and the caveats

Triage · Bitext customer support

Pick 1 of 11 support categories for a customer message. 18 per category.

Intent + out-of-scope · CLINC150

Pick 1 of 15 banking intents, or out_of_scope. 150 in-scope cases plus 50 out-of-scope: 25 generic and 25 near-miss credit-card requests.

Toxicity guardrail · Civil Comments

Yes/no: is the comment toxic? 100 toxic and 100 non-toxic, using the standard 0.5 annotator cut-off.

Tool selection · BFCL v3 “multiple”

Pick the right function out of 2–4 candidates. Needle also fills in the arguments, scored against BFCL's accepted answers.

How to read it

  • Auto @95%: the share of all cases that could be handled with no human, by trusting the most-confident answers first while staying ≥95% correct.
  • Calibration error (ECE): the average gap between a model's stated confidence and how often it's actually right. Lower is better.
  • When Needle declines to answer, that counts as wrong.

Caveats

  • Latency isn't a like-for-like comparison. Jev's time includes the internet round trip. Open-Jev runs on the Mac GPU without the fast code it has on NVIDIA. Needle runs on the CPU.
  • Needle was given each question as a tool to call. Its strength is tool calls and extraction, not judgement.
  • One run of about 200 cases per task: differences of 2–3 points are within noise.
